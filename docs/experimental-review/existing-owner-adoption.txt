Existing-owner adoption check

Within each selected semantic family, when a cohesive shared owner already exists or is introduced, inspect relevant sibling implementations and callers for remaining implementations of the same responsibility. Compare the actual ordered collaborator operations, inputs, results and mutation targets; similar surface syntax alone is insufficient. Identify already-adopted callers and residual implementations with exact source evidence, and distinguish intentional outer policy from the smaller shared knowledge. Before proposing reuse, preserve or explicitly mark uncertainty about guards, early exits, nil/empty behavior, newly eager field reads or argument evaluation, allocations, identity, errors, callbacks and resource lifetime. Characterize the existing observable boundary before editing. Record adoption, justified retention, unresolved evidence or unattempted work; do not invent an owner merely to manufacture a match, force a universal abstraction, or target complete adoption/count clearance.
