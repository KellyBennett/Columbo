# Ordinary-evidence composition pass

During untangling, also inspect the complete declaration queue selected by co-occurring unsuppressed non-PASS long-function and high-cognitive-complexity evidence in the full ordinary snapshot. The frozen query provides a reproducible inspection order, not an importance judgment or target metric. Keep every selected declaration's disposition and source-backed reason, including items not yet reached.

For each inspected declaration, read its whole source and collaborating context. Identify its concrete next-change burden and whether it coordinates independently understandable responsibilities. Try the smallest behavior-preserving composition boundary only when naming and owning that responsibility would reduce the burden enough to justify new indirection. Preserve genuinely different policies and observable evaluation, mutation, failure, lifecycle and callback order. Generated machinery, coherent algorithms and already useful owners may earn retention; explain the actual responsibility and why another boundary would cost more than it buys. Lack of a characterization test is an obligation to attempt, not a retention rationale.

All existing characterization, protected-test, full-suite, staged/ordinary snapshot and progress-checkpoint requirements continue. Metric reduction, shorter functions, lower indentation, extra helpers or stage clearance alone are not a gain. Record source-level ownership/change-locality evidence, added coordination costs and unresolved opportunities. This pass does not change analyzer gates, thresholds, suppressions or findings, and it does not replace the existing shared-responsibility queue.
