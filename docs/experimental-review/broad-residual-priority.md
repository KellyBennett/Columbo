# Broad residual-cost priority checkpoint

Before choosing the next production change, compare the substantial unresolved responsibilities already supported by the complete ordinary evidence and live review obligations. Include both previously changed families and untouched work. Do not stop at the first convenient extraction or compatible additional caller. A short function, a lower input count, ready tests or a likely metric reduction does not by itself make the next change more valuable.

Record a source-backed comparison: responsibility and evidence IDs; concrete coordinated-change, mutation, lifetime or policy burden; what prior changes actually reduced; what remains; available public characterization; expected owner/API cost; and why the next investigation ranks above the alternatives. Preserve justified cohesive retentions. Distinguish an unattempted responsibility from an unsuccessful attempt or a specific indispensable blocker. Do not manufacture responsibilities from every large function or treat every review lead as a required refactor.

Attempt characterization of the highest-value supported unresolved responsibility before falling back to a smaller cleanup merely because its tests are easier. Useful evidence gathering is progress even if no production edit is yet justified. When another choice is better, record the concrete reason and keep the deferred responsibility visible. Use original behavior, including existing defects, as the preservation contract unless a repair is separately authorized.

At the next checkpoint, report the actual choice, evidence attempted, learning, validated ownership benefit and remaining burden. Say whether a supervisor supplied the priority or the agent derived it from the frozen source/evidence. Preserve the previous choice and any intervention. Repeated small wins with unchanged substantial burdens require a new priority judgment; they do not establish convergence. Existing time/attempt triggers request supervision and sufficient validation reserve, never an automatic stop or an incentive to select only small edits. No collector, threshold, suppression, stage verdict or protected assertion changes.
