# Experimental source-backed review recipe

This is an experimental source-backed agent review procedure. It changes what the agent investigates,
not Columbo's collectors, stage membership, locking, thresholds, suppressions,
schema, commands or exit behavior. The [stage contract](refactoring-stages.md)
still determines actual tool and CI verdicts.

## Review obligations

1. Review in order: untangle behavior, assign ownership, consolidate shared
   responsibilities. Inspect full source context for every initial eligible ID;
   group related cases into semantic families without dropping IDs. Record a
   source-backed changed/validated, intentionally retained or unresolved
   disposition. Distinguish not reached at a stop from a reviewed retention.
   Preserve added and removed groups separately after fresh scans.
2. Prioritize meaningful repeated decision or execution protocols by shared
   knowledge, collaborating sites and coordinated next-change cost. Convenient
   formatting or conversion helpers are not a substitute for investigating the
   highest-value supported family. Preserve intentional differences. Do not
   introduce a pattern merely to give it a name.
3. Before editing, name the observable behavior boundary at risk and obtain useful
   characterization on unchanged code. Prefer portable external assertions that
   run unchanged before and after. New-private-helper-only tests are supplemental;
   implementation-derived expected values can be tautological. Preserve existing
   tests and assertions. A full-suite pass is not proof of equivalence.
4. Before declaring a family evidence-blocked, either attempt that observable
   characterization and retain its result, or identify a specific indispensable
   unavailable prerequisite and explain why it prevents a safe local oracle.
   Merely listing ordering, mutation, callback, failure or lifecycle risks does
   not meet this obligation. Failed or inconclusive evidence stays visible and
   does not force a production edit or authorize a new behavior policy.
5. Inspect eligible ownership groups and non-gating repeated-variant review leads.
   In the sharing phase, inspect every eligible duplicate-code group, including
   when earlier findings are retained or every tool stage is already clear. Seek
   shared responsibility rather than similar syntax; preserve repetition where
   contracts, lifetimes or change rates differ.
6. Make small changes. Record each hypothesis and checkpoint, then run the frozen
   full tests and fresh staged analysis each cycle. Focused characterization does
   not replace full validation. Preserve ordinary verdicts too. Keep original
   tests, dependencies, public behavior, analyzer configuration, thresholds,
   exclusions and suppressions unchanged. No syntax hiding, assertion weakening,
   target-zero rewrite or requirement to produce a patch is part of the recipe.

## Optional ordinary-evidence composition pass

The supervised discovery continuation also tested a separately frozen
[composition pass](experimental-review/composition-pass.md) with its exact
[SQLite selection query](experimental-review/composition-select.sql). It exposes
co-occurring non-PASS, unsuppressed long-function and cognitive-complexity evidence
as a complete declaration inspection queue. This is navigation for source review,
not semantic detection, a ranking of architectural importance or another gate.
Freeze the source, ordinary database and complete query result before beginning.
Retain every selected declaration, including those not yet reached, alongside the
existing shared-responsibility obligations. Record query and guidance hashes.

The [partial-declaration clarification](experimental-review/partial-declaration-residuals.md)
asks the worker to revisit the original responsibility after a validated boundary.
Close the specific cost that changed; keep only concrete remaining costs open.
Different policies need not be merged, but may still deserve distinct owners.
Neither an extraction nor a reviewed retention settles every responsibility in a
large declaration. Conversely, do not keep cohesive algorithms open indefinitely
merely because ordinary findings remain.

These exact instructions have exploratory outcome evidence: HCL gained separate
template, conditional/iteration, type-construction, diagnostic-rendering and
block-decoding responsibilities; PocketBase gained separate forward and inverse
relation traversal owners. Public-behavior characterization on unchanged starting
production and full-suite validation were required. The residual clarification
changed selection back toward unfinished responsibilities in already visited
owners. Added helper, parameter and coordination costs remain visible in ordinary
analysis. Independent full-source reviews of earlier checkpoints still show
substantial repository-wide costs. Those earlier reviews describe their pinned
checkpoints. Later continuations and their separate endpoint adjudications are
recorded below; their outcomes are not retrospectively attributed to these earlier
reviews or instructions.

HCL began this pass in a fresh worker. PocketBase, K9s and MinIO instead received it
in existing supervised workers, retaining earlier source and attempt context.
That reuse deviates from the original fresh-worker protocol clause; these are
exploratory continuations, not matched fresh-worker replications. No independent
review recommendations were supplied to those refactoring workers.

This is an experimental workflow option, not proven general efficacy or a claim
that the four repositories have reached the maintainability endpoint. It changes
no collector, schema, suppression, threshold, phase lock or exit code. Preserve
the progress-sensitive supervision and all validation/evidence obligations above.

## Optional existing-owner adoption check

The separately frozen [existing-owner adoption clause](experimental-review/existing-owner-adoption.txt)
asks whether a selected semantic family still contains another implementation of
knowledge already owned elsewhere. Its SHA-256 is
`a3537e30d3513528ba92b20cdfb2e032cad4852691ece0a7e181ee3bf1c99edd`.
Inventory adopted callers, residual implementations, justified retention,
unresolved evidence and work not reached. Compare actual collaborator order,
inputs/results, mutation targets and observable behavior before considering reuse.
This is a selected-family check, not a mandatory repository-wide sweep.

The earlier discovery pair supported useful bounded reuse but left most of its
preselected opportunities untouched. A later supervised continuation found
additional behavior-supported adoption and explicit incompatibility retentions.
These observations support experimental use, not a default gate, causal efficacy
claim or instruction to maximize the number of callers. Existing owners may need
to remain separate when lazy reads, failure phases, identity or lifetime differ.
Do not create a universal abstraction merely to make the inventory look complete.

At a priority checkpoint, compare concrete remaining costs from already changed
families with those from untouched entries. An open residual recorded only as text
has not participated in the decision. Preserve validated narrow boundaries and
cohesive retentions; compare only actual unresolved costs, then record why the next
attempt has the stronger expected ownership benefit and manageable coordination
cost. Useful characterization may justify retaining or deferring an edit.

## Review progression is not stage clearance

After every group in the current review phase has a disposition, the procedure
allows review of the next phase even when Columbo reports that stage as locked.
This does not change stage evaluation. For example, an active untangling stage
with two findings still locks ownership and consolidation even if those locked
stages display zero issues. A retained or unresolved finding remains an actual
finding, and an experimental disposition cannot turn it into CI success.

Staged output presents only active-stage issues. Use the complete snapshot and
ordinary analysis to retain the later-stage and duplicate evidence required by
the review. Preserve both actual verdicts and all locked states. Exit 1 means
findings; exit 2 or invalid/missing SQLite blocks analysis. All-clear bounded
collectors do not establish a well-factored repository.

## Budget, stops and evidence

For new supervised continuations, use the separately versioned
[progress-sensitive checkpoint policy](experimental-review/progress-checkpoints.md).
Freeze source, commands, protected tests and checkpoint triggers before inspection.
Time and attempt thresholds request supervisor judgment; they do not automatically
end useful investigation. Continue validated ownership improvements or concrete
characterization learning, and extend the validation allowance safely. Record all
elapsed time, failed attempts and queue costs without resetting them.

At each checkpoint, report actual attempts, observations, the current validated
endpoint, remaining obligations and one falsifiable next step. Repeated failed
approaches without new evidence call for redirection, worker replacement or
collector/phase investigation. Missing characterization is work to attempt, not
an automatic stopping reason. If the queue has no selected next step while material
source costs remain, inspect broader source evidence and diagnose the guidance
or detector gap. Closure requires supported endpoint quality and correctness, or
an exact indispensable blocker requiring input. Actual stage locks and CI verdicts
remain unchanged.

The frozen historical instruction files above still contain their original hard
stops. Preserve those bytes for reproducibility; their stopping rules are not the
policy used for the separately identified supervised continuations. That
policy is experimental and does not establish general efficacy by documentation
alone.

Retain the initial inventory, per-ID dispositions, cycle/failure ledger, source
diff and hashes, actual elapsed/remaining budget, stop reason, full validation,
stage and ordinary verdicts, and remaining obligations. The frozen assignment
names the exact report artifacts. Report only available model/settings/usage
metadata; do not invent tokens, backend identity or cost. Do not feed independent
reviewer recommendations or another intervention's output into a blinded run.

## Evidence and limits

The PocketBase characterization follow-on produced one further validated shared
protocol owner: 26 inline model-event synchronization implementations became four
typed implementations. Its independent source review accepted those typed
boundaries. Earlier combined-recipe HCL and MinIO replays also produced bounded
gains while retaining active/locked tool states. The characterization amendment
must not be credited retrospectively to those earlier replays.

These are exposed discovery experiments, not a controlled causal prompt study,
universal success rate or proof of architectural completion. Substantial repeated
responsibilities remained at that historical checkpoint. That PocketBase run
characterized transaction completion
and retained it without trying a shared completion owner; the new oracle did not
show that sharing would be harmful. Source-supported retention and valid tests do
not by themselves demonstrate that useful work is exhausted.

### Historical next hypothesis, outside the frozen recipe

At that historical checkpoint, a proposed stronger post-characterization
decision rule would separate a repeated
invariant from caller-specific policy and evaluate the smallest shared owner

That stronger rule was not validated by those results and is not included in
the frozen instructions or shipped stage tasks. It required a separately bounded
experiment; it must not be reported as an improvement already tested by that
historical recipe. Later owner investigations and endpoint judgments do not
establish universal shared-owner efficacy.
